## Frozen experiment design

Study `unseen-baostock-20261116` uses three previously unused research symbols:
600000.XSHG, 600519.XSHG and 000651.XSHE, 3 August–30 October 2026. This is an
operator-selected convenience sample, not a representative or point-in-time A-share
universe. The data are current downloads of historical, unadjusted daily closes.
No factor-performance conclusion, corporate-action classification or tradability
claim is supported by close-only evidence. Missing market dates cannot be diagnosed
as suspension without a separate calendar/status source. No other data vendor is used.

Download one bounded chunk first, check identity/schema/rows, then finish the six
predeclared chunks sequentially. Reuse the exact frozen snapshot in both arms.
No source scope, ticker, date or acceptance threshold is selected from model outcomes.
The initial five-observation return formula is correct and has no planted defect.
The case has no answer key or prescribed final factor. Models may reject revision;
that is a recorded checkpoint outcome, not a reason to manufacture a correction.

Both arms get the same initial candidate, full evidence, two worker roles, native
DSH tools, model settings and ceilings: 32 requests, six worker activations,
six factor inspections, 16384 output tokens per request. Actual work and tokens
may differ. The initial Flash amendment to 240 requests counts all historical GLM
calls and retained its 1500-credit/20 CNY local bounds. The owner-directed GLM 5.3
amendment below changed those local bounds before any GLM 5.3 request.

- Fixed workflow: evidence analyst → factor designer → final analyst review.
  The sequence and assignments are frozen. A failed final review ends incomplete.
- Adaptive workflow: the supervisor chooses assignments and additional bounded
  follow-ups from observed findings/reviews. It must obtain evidence analysis,
  a concrete candidate and a review of the exact final version.
- First pair runs fixed then adaptive. If both can be audited and usage settles,
  repeat with fresh sessions in reverse order. These are small counterbalanced
  mechanism demonstrations, not a statistically powered superiority experiment.

Factor definitions use a restricted executable specification: trailing return
lookback/direction, optional trailing realized-volatility normalization and an
optional past-return eligibility bound. The host calculates exact specified scores
without evaluating model code. All inputs at signal close t are current/past;
execution is no earlier than the next observed close. The inspection tool returns
descriptive coverage/score calculations, not backtest labels or an evaluation service.

## Acceptance and recording

Accept a capability demonstration only when native tasks completed, the final
candidate has a source-linked substantive change with a peer finding and explicit
decision, executable scores or eligibility actually differ, and an exact-version
review plus independent arithmetic/semantic audit supports the explanation.
Revision alone is not evidence of improvement, alpha or collaboration advantage.
Keep unchanged candidates, model failures and false review passes distinct.

Retain source/config/plan hashes before dispatch; all data attempts and transport
receipts; row-quality checks; native assignments, inspections, findings, parentage,
decisions and reviews; raw model requests/responses; settled/reserved exposure;
per-actor tokens, RPM/TPM/cache; CPU/RAM/swap/disk observations; failed attempts;
and every protocol amendment. Public evidence excludes credentials and bootstrap
URLs. Existing fixed-scope trials and completed diagnostic packages remain immutable.

## Fixes and GLM 5.3 amendment

The Flash attempt exposed exact-citation and submission-contract errors. Error
messages now identify the source/index and distinguish decoded literal text from
escaped strings. Schemas clarify initial versus repair resolutions, ordinary limits
versus blocking defects, and consistent verdict/issues fields. Generic guidance
separates an observed fact, an exploratory hypothesis and an unsupported exclusivity
claim. No fuzzy citation acceptance or forced factor correction was introduced.
Finding batches validate before any append, failed native tasks refuse paid replay,
and Python workers import the server's immutable source snapshot.

The native Models page now pins the case package's selected model. The GLM 5.3
route remains `zai-coding-cn` on the existing Coding Plan Pro credential, with low
reasoning effort, thinking enabled and concurrency one. Cash exposure and credit
reservations use GLM 5.3 rates rather than inherited Flash rates. Local bounds are
240 aggregate historical requests, 4000 conservative credits and a 40 CNY package
sublimit inside the unchanged 200 CNY total. They are operator caps, not a reading
of remaining provider quota. No metered fallback is allowed.

The guard uses 8 CNY/million input tokens and 28 CNY/million output tokens, ignoring
cache discounts; its credit estimate uses 690/2400 microcredits per input/output
token, ignoring cache/off-peak discounts. Rates were verified on 16 November against
the [official pricing](https://docs.bigmodel.cn/cn/guide/start/pricing) and
[Coding Plan coefficients](https://docs.bigmodel.cn/cn/coding-plan/overview).
Full-context plus 16384-output admission reserves 8.847360 CNY and 762.839040 credits
per request, then settles against reported usage. These are conservative exposure
quantities, not incremental subscription bills.

Both model and contract guidance changed after the Flash attempt. The later result
cannot isolate the causal effect of the stronger model. Within the GLM 5.3 comparison,
all 120 frozen source files and the question/evidence/initial definition/model/limits
match exactly. After live testing stopped, only failure-export handling and its
offline regression were added; live snapshots remain unchanged.

This proves arithmetic, not an interpretation, improved factor or profitable alpha.
The two completed GLM 5.3 candidates are defensible **retentions with limits**.
Neither proves that all alternatives are invalid. The team sometimes treats lack of
proven improvement as a reason against exploration; an early supported hypothesis
does not need profitable alpha to warrant investigation.

No GLM 5.3 attempt changed the factor or applicability. An already coherent initial
formula and close-only evidence may make this a weak stimulus for evolution. The
substantive adaptive-revision gate remains open. Do not force a revision to pass it.
One pair cannot establish collaboration superiority or model superiority.

## Timeout and stop rule

Adaptive 03 started at 07:55:05 UTC / 15:55:05 Beijing. DSH reported a 300-second
stream-idle timeout. The guard continued accounting independently and ended at
08:12:23 UTC / 16:12:23 Beijing with `ConnectTimeout`, before any HTTP receipt.
There is no evidence that this attempt was processed or billed upstream, but no
usage receipt proves zero charge. It remains **unknown**, with its full cash/credit
reservation held. The local evidence cannot identify provider outage versus a local
network/TLS problem; this is not evidence of insufficient model intelligence.

The frozen repeat rule and independent ledger guard stop further dispatch on
uncertain charges. `unseen-glm53-fixed-04` was therefore not run. The three unused
Flash packages were already superseded by the owner's model switch. None were
silently retried or substituted. Further live work requires supported reconciliation
of this request and diagnosis of the timeout path; a timeout cannot be reclassified
as the earlier reviewed 401 to bypass the guard.

failure. Its error screenshot was manually inspected and retained; this automation
failure is separate from the native timeout. Desktop/reload checks passed for the
completed runs. Native DSH clipping at 390px remains; mobile is not accepted.
