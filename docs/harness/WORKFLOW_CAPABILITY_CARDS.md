# Workflow capability cards

Evidence reviewed on 16 November 2026. These cards describe observed mechanisms,
not investment performance. The first six variants used one synthetic diagnostic
with a withheld answer key. Equal evidence and output ceilings did not make actual
compute equal. The native BaoStock case used real observations, a correct initial
factor and no prescribed final answer. An unchanged factor is not a new generation.

## Single agent

- Evidence: three one-call successes in [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md).
- Demonstrated: extract and combine the diagnostic timing/unit constraints with
  access to the complete evidence bundle.
- Limits: one easy case, no distributed-information advantage to overcome and no
  open-ended discovery claim. Its lower cost is specific to this comparison.
- Suitable use: baseline for bounded tasks that fit one context.
- Next experiment: use the same unseen evidence and candidate as a collaborative
  arm, with a separately declared compute-matched allowance.

## Blackboard collaboration

- Evidence: three three-call successes in [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md);
  immutable findings/parentage in [the state module](MODULAR_DEVELOPMENT_2026-11-13.md).
- Demonstrated: one worker's cited contribution can be retained and consumed by
  a later worker; the host preserves versions, decisions and conflicts.
- Limits: no accuracy gain over the single agent on the diagnostic. Structural
  provenance does not prove that a finding is scientifically correct.
- Suitable use: preserve shared findings that need attribution and later revision.
- Next experiment: a real case with complementary evidence and a board-disabled
  aggregation control; check which contribution changed the final definition.

## Independent work and aggregation

- Evidence: one three-call success in [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md).
- Demonstrated: independently produced contributions can enter a final synthesis.
- Limits: one synthetic case; true concurrent execution, durable branch recovery
  and preservation of genuinely distinct research ideas are not demonstrated.
- Suitable use: control for whether interaction contributes beyond aggregation.
- Next experiment: repeat with competing source-supported hypotheses and audit
  which evidence and dissent survive synthesis.

## Self revision

- Evidence: one three-call success in [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md).
- Demonstrated: an agent can re-examine its own diagnostic proposal within a bound.
- Limits: no demonstrated advantage over the one-call baseline; a revised message
  alone is not evidence of a corrected factor.
- Suitable use: low-coordination control for peer-review experiments.
- Next experiment: compare with peer review on the same initial artifact, recording
  corrected defects, introduced defects and actual compute.

## Iterative peer review

- Evidence: [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md), paired timing/unit experiments in
  [the module record](MODULAR_DEVELOPMENT_2026-11-13.md), and targeted repairs in
  [completed-output review](FINAL_OUTPUT_REVIEW_2026-11-13.md).
- Demonstrated: exact-version feedback can cause a targeted explanation amendment;
  a clean control can skip rewriting. Source-derived unit checks caught a model's
  false pass and the two retained counterexamples were repaired.
- Limits: the initial unit explanations failed in both feedback arms. The host
  grammar is deliberately narrow; a clean model verdict is not semantic proof.
- Suitable use: bounded inspection and repair with immutable originals and an
  independently checkable acceptance condition.
- Next experiment: extend the semantic counterexample set and exercise a justified
  computational or applicability change, not just explanation repair.

## Debate

- Evidence: one three-call success in [the first pilot](FIRST_PILOT.md).
- Demonstrated: proposer/challenger contributions can be passed to a synthesizer.
- Limits: no advantage over controls on the single diagnostic, no broader repeats,
  and no evidence that longer debate or agreement predicts research quality.
- Suitable use: a hypothesis with a concrete, source-checkable disputed assumption.
- Next experiment: compare debate with independent analyses on a new case; require
  the challenge to change a recorded decision without inventing a defect.

## Fixed specialist handoffs

- Evidence: [the unseen BaoStock case](UNSEEN_CASE_2026-11-16.md), using a frozen
  analyst → designer → exact-version-review sequence.
- Demonstrated: native DSH child tasks produce cited findings, an executable
  candidate or explicit retention, and a review pinned to that version. The host
  stops rather than bypassing a failed final review.
- Limits: the first Flash run changed scores but its explanation failed review.
  The GLM 5.3 runs and failures are recorded separately. A fixed sequence cannot
  request a repair after its final review within this control's protocol.
  In the [maximum-effort control](MAX_EFFORT_2026-11-16.md), the designer inspected
  a normalization hypothesis but deferred it. Citation/parent repairs used the
  five-request allowance; the accepted retention lacked a native terminal
  acknowledgement and never reached review. Inspection is not adoption.
- Suitable use: equal-evidence control for adaptive assignments.
- Next experiment: retain the fixed stage assignments while changing the research
  case under a new predeclared protocol; do not tune the control after seeing results.

## Adaptive supervisor with two worker roles

- Evidence: targeted native follow-ups in [completed-output review](FINAL_OUTPUT_REVIEW_2026-11-13.md)
  and [the unseen BaoStock comparison](UNSEEN_CASE_2026-11-16.md).
- Demonstrated: the supervisor selects assignments from observed findings/reviews,
  attributes decisions and obtains review of the current candidate. Two worker
  role types are reused through fresh native child activations; this is not a
  claim of exactly two persistent child sessions.
- Limits: the unseen GLM 5.3 case has not demonstrated a substantive adaptive factor
  revision. The correct starting formula and close-only evidence may be weak
  stimuli for evolution. Contract repairs and a transport timeout remain recorded.
  A defensible retention decision does not establish general discovery capability.
  Maximum effort used substantially more output tokens but again retained the
  baseline; its reviewer approved an incorrect count explanation. The supervisor
  narrowed later assignments around the initial analyst's conclusion. These are
  observed failure mechanisms, not a controlled proof that effort or supervision
  generally reduces quality. Tool-result truncation also exposed an export gap;
  native session-event auditing now verifies actual task states independently.
- Suitable use: provisional research core for bounded, auditable development.
- Next experiment: a new frozen case with an evidence-supported applicability
  constraint or competing testable specifications. Permit justified exploratory
  hypotheses without requiring profitable alpha; do not prescribe a correction.

## Core decision and module contracts

Keep the native DSH supervisor with author and reviewer role types as the
**provisional** core because delegation, attributed state and exact-version review
are inspectable. Do not declare the minimal-core capability gate complete until a
source-supported substantive adaptive revision survives independent review and a
same-evidence fixed control. No measured universal collaboration advantage is claimed.

The first module contracts are already exercised separately: shared state accepts
a current parent plus cited findings and records every disposition; completed-output
review accepts an immutable artifact and returns defects/limits against its exact
version; amendments retain the original and require a new review. The restricted
factor-specification module adds deterministic score/eligibility calculations and
independent arithmetic, but does not expose an evaluator or publish results.

Next isolate parent-to-child factor evolution and released-report matching, then
integrate one accepted loop. Parallel research, frozen-pool curation and composition
need their own module fixtures and failure records before Hybrid v2. Timeout
reconciliation, operational recovery and broader source/semantic tests remain
production work, separate from the early research-capability gate.

Before another open-ended comparison, repair compact/paged board delivery and
terminal acknowledgement accounting, add the count/prose counterexamples, separate
exploration from adoption criteria, and isolate the research workspace from unrelated
Git edits. Keep the original 200 CNY ledger and unresolved reservations intact.
